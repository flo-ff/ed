Film makes me **wait**. Twelve, twenty-four, thirty-six frames — you don't spray, you watch. The grain, the missed focus, the light leak — that's not a mistake, that's the city showing through.

Digital lets me keep going when the light is gone, when the trick needs ten tries, when a stranger only gives you half a second. No waiting for the lab to see if it worked.
